# Capital Emergency AA / Advanced Infantry Balance Pass

- Capital now has innate emergency anti-air defense: 400 range, 4 homing Air-Defense-style missiles per salvo, 4s salvo cooldown.
- Emergency Capital AA uses the existing Air-Defense projectile damage/speed/homing rules and inherits the Missile Doctrine proximity-fuse capability when researched.
- Capital takes 20% less air-delivered damage. This applies to Bomber bombs, Fighter A2G missiles, Loyal Wingman strikes, and Loitering Munitions; strategic MLS missiles are unchanged.
- Advanced Infantry regeneration reduced to Healing 2% max HP/s and Immortal 4% max HP/s; both together restore 6%/s.
- Exosuit equipment prices increased by tier: Basic-tier equipment +$2 over the prior price, Elite-tier equipment +$4 over the prior price, and shared weapons +$2. Final catalog surcharges are Basic helmet/body/boots $4, Elite helmet/body/boots $8, shared weapons $6, Basic modules $7, Elite modules $14.
