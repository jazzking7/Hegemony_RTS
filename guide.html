FIELD GUIDE / SINGLE-PLAYER + MULTIPLAYER

Build. Hold. Advance.

Protect your Capital and destroy every rival Capital to win. Capturing territory funds your army; it does not win the match by itself.

  1. Grow income. Build Cities inside your construction radius. Select a City and choose Upgrade Economy for +50% income per level, up to level 5.
  2. Train a force. Select a Military Base and train ground units. Completed troops stay stored until you deploy them.
  3. Take territory. Click a capture zone and choose Deploy to This Zone, or point at it and press A. Choose your source, enter counts, then confirm.
  4. Choose a doctrine. Press R, start a doctrine, then research the specific units or buildings you want in its tree.
  5. Keep your Capital safe. Add defenses and send attacks toward rival Capitals. Aircraft, missiles, and automation add options as you research them.

Controls & basic rules

Move around

Drag the map or use WASD / arrow keys. Use the mouse wheel to zoom. Click your buildings to inspect them. Press Esc to cancel the current action or close a panel.

Build & deploy

Hover the left BUILD strip, choose a structure, then click a valid location. The menu folds away during placement; hover the strip to reopen it. Continuous Build repeats placement until cancelled.

A opens deployment at the pointer. R opens research. T opens missile aiming when available. Space pauses the simulation; in multiplayer, pause is shared.

Economy & limits

Your living Capital supplies $100/min. City upgrades compound by 1.5×. Income arrives continuously; power systems can add operating costs. Military Bases hold up to 80 ground units and queue up to 60. An Airbase initially maintains 7 manned aircraft across ready, returning, airborne, and queued aircraft.

Multiplayer

Up to 5 commanders share a lobby, counting humans and AI. Every human must be ready before the host starts. Orders take effect on synchronized ticks, so save confirmation may arrive shortly after clicking. There are no alliances or shared territory incomes.

TERRITORY / INCOME

Capture zones & regions

Standard maps have 16 zones in 6 regions; large maps have 25 in 8. Every zone has radius 60. Zone centers are at least 200 apart. Matching letters and connecting lines identify a region.

Capture a neutral zone

Overlap it with a ground unit or building for 20 seconds. More troops do not speed capture. Aircraft, flying drones, stored troops, and embarked or garrisoned troops do not count separately.

If a rival enters, capture pauses until one player remains. Your progress resets when all your eligible entities leave or die. Empty neutral zones remain neutral.

Attack an owned zone

Any rival presence immediately makes the zone contested and stops its income. The owner has 10 seconds to clear the invaders. Clearing them in time preserves ownership.

After 10 seconds of continuous invasion, ownership is revoked. A lone remaining player then needs a fresh 20-second capture. Multiple players keep it contested; nobody left means neutral.

Leave a zone

An uncontested owned zone stays yours for 60 seconds while empty, continuing to earn income and qualify for its region bonus. Returning resets that countdown. When it expires, the zone becomes neutral.

Earn the region bonus

Each uncontested owned zone pays $500/min. One player owning every uncontested zone in a region receives an additional $100/min per zone.

A five-zone region earns $2,500 + $500 = $3,000/min. Contesting even one zone suspends that zone’s income and the entire region bonus; the other uncontested zones still pay.

LEARN ONE SYSTEM AT A TIME

Short tutorials

Train and deploy ground troops
  1. Select a Military Base; choose a unit and train it. Watch queue progress and stored counts update live.
  2. Point at your destination and press A. Use a single facility or Global Overview → Ground to combine inventories.
  3. Enter counts and confirm. Units travel toward the destination and engage enemies. A Military Command enables MAX / 10 / 5 / 1 controls and convoy definitions.
Define and use a convoy
  1. Build and select a Military Command. Name one of its three convoy slots and enter the troop counts.
  2. Click Save Convoy. In multiplayer, wait for confirmation; your numbers remain visible while waiting.
  3. At a Military Base, queue the saved convoy, or add its composition in the deployment dialog. Saving a definition alone does not train or deploy troops.
Research efficiently
  1. Press R. The availability badge shows free slots; your Capital starts with two.
  2. Choose a doctrine first, then follow its specialized research prerequisites. Starting another doctrine doubles its base price: $500, $1,000, $2,000, and so on.
  3. Doctrine research takes 100 seconds before modifiers. Most specialized research starts at $100 / 60 seconds; some advanced entries cost more. The displayed price and duration include applicable modifiers.
  4. Advanced Engineering can unlock Research Centers for additional shared slots. Unlocking a building makes it appear in the build menu; it does not place it for you.
Operate an Airbase
  1. Train a Bomber, or research Fighters / Paratrooper Planes first. All manned aircraft share the Airbase’s fleet limit.
  2. Use A → Air or select an individual Airbase to launch ready aircraft.
  3. Returning aircraft need turnaround before another sortie. Fighter missile and paratrooper passenger configurations are saved at the Airbase; existing aircraft keep their relevant stored configuration.
Schedule several deployments
  1. Complete Advanced Command & Automation, then open the Scheduled Deployment editor at a Military Command.
  2. Fill up to three nodes and the waits between them; save the schedule. Train Required Units fills missing production requirements.
  3. Open deployment at the desired target and launch the schedule. Each node waits for its full force; a wait starts after the preceding node launches.
Set up Automated Warfare
  1. Research Automated Warfare in the Command doctrine and build an Advanced Coordination Center.
  2. Select a ground facility for a Theater Routine, or an Airbase for aerial automation. Choose a definition and enter a non-empty force.
  3. Enter the target coordinates or use Pick on Map. Initiate the routine with enough money for its fee and free bandwidth.
  4. Stored units are used before new production. Failed creation keeps your entries and explains the problem. Pause or cancel existing routines at the center. Paratrooper Planes cannot join Area Denial.
Save an Exosuit loadout
  1. Complete Advanced Infantry, research the Superior Mobilization Complex, and build it.
  2. Choose a soldier type, equipment, weapons, and modules; give the loadout a unique name and save.
  3. Train the saved loadout at an eligible facility, then deploy it by name. New saves do not retroactively change units already built. Clear starts a new definition; Edit modifies a saved one.
Use missiles, engineers, and power
  1. Missiles: research and build a Missile Launch Site, configure silo loads, build stockpile missiles, then enter aiming mode and confirm a valid target.
  2. Engineers: unlock and build an Engineering Complex, train Engineers, and let them dispatch for nearby work. Later research enables automatic replenishment and onsite upgrades.
  3. Power: build generators near energy consumers, set output and priorities, and check supply before relying on an orbital system, field, or Death Ray. Their upkeep reduces net income.
