COMMAND RADIUS / FIELD GUIDE

Build. Hold. Advance.

★ Protect your Capital. Destroy every rival Capital to win.

All controls, economy & multiplayer limits

Move & command

Drag / WASD / arrows: pan · Wheel: zoom · Click: inspect · A: deploy at pointer · R: research · T: missiles · Esc: cancel / close · Space: pause · H: quick read.

Hover BUILD → choose structure → click valid location. Continuous Build repeats until cancelled. During placement, hover the left strip to reopen the menu.

Economy & capacity

Living Capital: $100/min. City upgrades: ×1.5 income per level, up to level 5. Income flows continuously; power upkeep reduces net income.

Military Base: 80 stored troops / 60 queued. Airbase: initially 7 manned aircraft total, including ready, returning, airborne and queued.

Research

Capital: 2 slots. Doctrine base cost doubles each time one starts: $500 → $1,000 → $2,000. Base time: 100s. Most specialized research: $100 / 60s; some entries cost more. Live prices include modifiers.

Research Centers add shared slots after the required unlocks. Unlocking a building makes it available to build; it does not place it.

Multiplayer

Up to 5 commanders, counting humans and AI. Every human must be ready before the host starts. Pause is shared. Orders and saves take effect on synchronized ticks; wait for confirmation.

No alliances or shared territory income.

Capture zones

Capture rules & edge cases

Presence

Ground units and buildings count, including edge overlap. Radius: 60. Aircraft, flying drones, stored, embarked and garrisoned troops do not count separately. More troops do not speed capture.

Rivals pause neutral capture. Losing all your eligible presence resets your progress. Empty neutral zones stay neutral.

Invaded or empty?

Rival entry immediately stops zone income and the region bonus. Clear every rival within 10s to keep ownership; otherwise ownership is revoked. One remaining player starts a fresh 20s capture; multiple players keep it contested.

An owned, empty zone stays yours and earns income for 60s. Returning resets the countdown.

Region income

Each uncontested zone: $200/min. Own every uncontested zone in a region: extra N × $100/min.

Five zones: 5 × $200 + 5 × $100 = $1,500/min. A contested zone loses its income and the region bonus; other zones still pay.

Territorial Control

Owned polygon area ÷ whole map area. Ownership remains during grace periods until revoked. This never extends construction ranges: only Capitals and Fortresses provide buildable zones.

Standard: 16 zones / 6 regions. Large: 25 / 8. Zone centers stay at least 200 apart. Territory Details shows region borders, owners and timers; matching letters identify a region.

SET IT UP / SEND IT OUT

Short tutorials

Choose a system for the exact steps.

Train and deploy ground troops
  1. Select Military Base → choose unit → train. Watch the queue and stored count.
  2. Point at destination → A. Choose a facility, or Global Overview → Ground to combine inventories.
  3. Enter counts → confirm. A Military Command enables MAX / 10 / 5 / 1 controls and convoy definitions.
Define and use a convoy
  1. Select Military Command → name one of three slots → enter counts.
  2. Save Convoy. In multiplayer, wait for confirmation; numbers remain while waiting.
  3. Queue the convoy at a base, or add it in deployment. Saving alone does not train or deploy.
Research efficiently
  1. R → check free slots → choose doctrine.
  2. Follow prerequisite arrows to the desired upgrade. Each doctrine started doubles the next doctrine's base price.
  3. Unlock Research Centers through Advanced Engineering for more slots. Build unlocked facilities yourself.
Operate an Airbase
  1. Train Bombers; research Fighters / Paratrooper Planes first. All manned aircraft share the fleet limit.
  2. A → Air, or select an Airbase → launch ready aircraft.
  3. Returning aircraft need turnaround. Save fighter missiles / paratrooper passengers at the Airbase; existing aircraft retain their relevant stored configuration.
Schedule several deployments
  1. Complete Command doctrine → Military Command → Scheduled Deployment.
  2. Fill up to three nodes and waits → save. Train Required Units fills missing production.
  3. Open deployment at target → launch schedule. Each node waits for its full force; waits begin after the preceding launch.
Set up Automated Warfare
  1. Research Automated Warfare → build Advanced Coordination Center.
  2. Select a ground facility for Theater Routine, or an Airbase for aerial automation. Choose definition → enter a non-empty force.
  3. Enter coordinates / Pick on Map → Initiate. Needs fee + free bandwidth. Stored units are used first.
  4. Pause / cancel at the center. Failed saves keep entries and explain why. Paratrooper Planes cannot join Area Denial.
Save an Exosuit loadout
  1. Complete Advanced Infantry → research and build Superior Mobilization Complex.
  2. Choose soldier, equipment, weapons and modules → unique name → save.
  3. Train at an eligible facility → deploy by name. Existing troops keep their loadouts. Clear creates a new definition; Edit changes a saved one.
Use missiles, engineers, and power
  1. Missiles: unlock / build Launch Site → configure silos → produce stockpile → aim → confirm valid target.
  2. Engineers: unlock / build Engineering Complex → train Engineers for nearby work. Later research adds replenishment and onsite upgrades.
  3. Power: generators near consumers → output + priority → check supply, charge and upkeep before using beams or fields.
